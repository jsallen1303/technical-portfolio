Pictures from many of these high-energy astrophysics missions are available from the HEASARC’s “Pretty Pictures” page.

Color codes indicate completed/past (blue), active/current (green), and future (gold). Missions tagged with “HEASARC” indicate missions where catalogs and/or data are available (or, for future missions, will be available) at the HEASARC.

The time range of these missions can also be explored on the time ordered mission launch date charts and the energy range is shown on the energy range charts.
